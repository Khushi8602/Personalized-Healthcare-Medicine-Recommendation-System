How to read these numbers

  • Top-1 means the first guess is right. Top-3 means the right disease is among the three best guesses.
  • The project keeps only one machine learning model (the best of the ones tried) and one deep learning model. The app shows both, and ranks diseases by the average of their probabilities.
  • All three scores are close to guessing. With {{ m.n_diseases }} diseases, around 3 patients each, and only 8 inputs, the data cannot separate them. The gaps between the models are within normal run-to-run variation, so none of them is clearly better.
  • Scores were measured on unique rows only, with 5-fold cross-validation. Counting repeated rows would inflate them.
  • That is why the form lets a patient pick a known diagnosis. Medicines and care tips then depend on that diagnosis, not on the weak guess.
  • Risk level is nearly fixed per disease in this file, so it is looked up from the disease.
